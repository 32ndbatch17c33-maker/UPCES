Registry Information & Disclosures

Technical architecture specifications registry for validation audits.

Official Designation: UPCES Scorer Dashboard

Target Society: University of the Philippines Civil Engineering Society (UPCES)

Functional Objective: Designed to modernly track, aggregate, and report evaluation points securely during marquee flagship competition cycles such as AutoMATHIC and PaTVyan.

Access Control: Strictly limited to verified UPCES alumni, active executive event organizers, and chapter residents holding a system-generated authentication passkey.

• Data Resiliency: If a scorer accidentally signs out, clears cache, or loses browser state, re-entering the unique assignment passkey instantly reconciles and pulls all previously tracked item selections straight down from the network node without data dropouts.

• Hot-Swapping Credentials: Assigned registry scorers can be dynamically changed or modified over the server pipeline mid-event without altering or wiping the historical score arrays already configured for that target team unit.

• Offline Interoperability: Network interruptions will safely retain structural mutations locally until the client establishes connection verification with the central database endpoint again.

• Confidentiality Matrix: All score yields and metric tallies are treated as confidential, handled safely in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Deployment Model: 100% Free platform architecture with zero usage tiers or operational costs.

Scalability Index: Crafted to be fully reusable across successive future UPCES organizational cycle architectures.

Security Protocol: Unauthorized redistribution, visual exposure, or leaking of scoring dashboards outside assigned evaluation marshals is strictly prohibited under local data governance protocols.
